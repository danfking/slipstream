# Live demo

*(Switch to the app. Everything runs against synthetic data, and I will say so on screen. The arc is one morning at the marina: what came in, can we keep up, what's stuck, what it's costing, prove the answers, then act.)*

1. **Morning briefing:** overnight activity, this week's biggest risk, and the number to act on today, with receipts alongside.
2. **What came in overnight:** "Anything come in overnight?" Bookings and an engine problem phoned in through the Blu Voice Agent. The partner answers the phone, the record keeps the truth, and this screen is where it all becomes visible.
3. **Can the yard keep up:** "Can we keep up with the haul-out schedule over the next three weeks?" Answer: 54 hours of demand against 38 of capacity in week two.
4. **What's stuck:** "Which jobs are blocked waiting on parts? Flag any high-priority ones." Six jobs, delivery dates against schedule dates, the charter customer flagged.
5. **What it's costing:** "How much completed work haven't we billed yet?" Answer: $16,419 across 14 jobs. The revenue-leakage headline.
6. **A preview of the Advisor tier:** the input is already suggesting the next question, in the context of this conversation. Press Tab to accept it and send. The suggestions are designed to only ask what the tools can answer, and their take-up is measured; that number is the evidence for when the Advisor tier is ready to sell.
7. **The golden batch, run live:** the accuracy checks running in front of you, against the record as it stands, before we change it.
8. **The approval gate, to finish:** "Draft invoices for all the unbilled completed work." Approve. Then "Check the record again: how much completed work is still unbilled?" The number moves to zero, and the approval has its own receipt. The model cannot write anything on its own.

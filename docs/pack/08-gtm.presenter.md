# Go-to-market

### Positioning

- Making the marina's operations answerable, sold to the GM and owner

### Landing it

- The install base is the distribution: 650 marinas, an expansion conversation, no cold outreach
- Beta at a few large full-service yards, general launch at the user conference

### Pricing and the number

- Per-marina monthly, three tiers along the stages
- Anchored to recovered hours and dollars found, a few of either covers it
- The base alone: about $230k to $470k a year, assumptions on the slide

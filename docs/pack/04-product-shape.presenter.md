# Product shape: three stages, each one sellable

- Assistant: answers what the yard already asks, read-only
- Advisor: brings the findings before you ask, still read-only
- Agent teams: act on findings, always behind a person's approval
- Each stage sells on its own and earns the trust the next one needs

### Who pays, who uses

- Buyer: the GM or owner, who sees the P&L
- Daily user: the service manager
- I have shipped this progression before: assistant, then insights, then agents

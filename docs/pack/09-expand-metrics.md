# Scale: marine proves the pattern, the group replays it

### Beyond the base

First beyond DockMaster's install base to the wider marine-software market — the same playbook the group has already run in other verticals: incubate a venture on one portfolio company's distribution, then sell past it.

### The group-level asset

The building blocks are not marine-specific. A typed tool layer over a system of record, composable domain agents behind human approval, a golden-batch accuracy harness, provider-agnostic serving — every one of the group's ~170 vertical-software companies sits on a system of record this pattern applies to. **Marine proves the pattern; the group replays it.** That, not the marina product alone, is the venture.

### What gets measured

- **Weekly active GMs and service managers** — is it a habit or a demo?
- **Answered-question rate** — how often the tools could actually answer what was asked
- **Attach rate against the 15-30% assumption** — the GTM model, measured
- **Feedback scores** on answers
- **Dollars saved or realised per marina** — the customer's ROI, and the renewal argument, in one number

The product instruments its own next stage: suggestion-adoption telemetry (which proactive prompts users act on) is the measured gate for introducing the Advisor tier and its cross-sell moment.

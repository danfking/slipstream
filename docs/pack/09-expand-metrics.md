# Scale: marine proves the pattern, the group replays it

### Beyond the install base

The next step is the wider marine-software market, using the approach the group has already used elsewhere: build a venture on one portfolio company's customers, then sell it beyond them.

### The asset the group keeps

Nothing in the building blocks is marine-specific. A tool layer over a system of record, domain agents behind human approval, an accuracy harness, and provider-independent serving would apply to any of the group's ~170 software companies, because every one of them sits on a system of record. Marine proves the pattern, and the group can replay it. That reuse, more than the marina product alone, is what makes this a venture worth funding.

### What gets measured

- **Weekly active GMs and service managers.** Is it becoming a habit, or was it just a demo?
- **Answered-question rate:** how often the tools could actually answer what was asked.
- **Take-up against the 15-30% assumption:** the sales model, measured against itself.
- **Feedback scores** on answers.
- **Dollars saved or found per marina:** the customer's return, and our renewal argument, in one number.

The product also measures its own next step: it tracks which suggested questions users actually use, and that take-up rate is the evidence for when to introduce the Advisor tier and offer the upgrade.
